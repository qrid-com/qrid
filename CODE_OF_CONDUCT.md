# Code of Conduct

The QRS community adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its Code of Conduct.

## Our pledge (summary)

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment‑free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio‑economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We expect everyone to be respectful, constructive and welcoming, to accept feedback gracefully and
to focus on what is best for the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, chat —
and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at **info@qrid.com**. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter will be respected.
Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).

The full text of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 is authoritative; this page is a summary.
